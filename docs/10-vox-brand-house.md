# 10 — VOX Brand House (reference)

Source: `VOX BRAND HOUSE_FINAL PPT_100124.pptx` (one-slide brand house, dated 10 Jan 2024), shared by Noorul on 8 Sep 2026 to keep on file for the VOX Cinemas Virtual Assistant's tone, copy and design decisions. Transcribed here so the agent prompt, widget copy and demo material can be checked against it.

## Why
- **Founding purpose:** We exist to capture hearts through the magic of big screen.
- **Vision:** To be the most loved and preferred brand where the heart of entertainment beats.
- **Communication objective:** To shift the mindset from *what's showing* to *what's happening*.
- **Essence:** **UNFORGETTABLY CINEMATIC**.
- **Beliefs — we believe:** in curating a happy space for people to come together; celebrating experiences and having fun is best shared; continuously evolving to provide the most immersive and "state of the art" big screen experiences; our customers are our guests, our team is our family, and our cinemas are our home.
- **Values:** Belonging · Happiness · Pioneering · Entertaining · Trusted.

## What
- **Character — The Everyday Superhero (Everyman/Hero):** superpower is turning the mundane into FUN and UNFORGETTABLE cinematic moments; a core desire for mastery, inspiring others to push themselves; strives to bring people together and create connections.
- **Design world — design language:** authentic, friendly, inclusive, conversational, fosters a sense of belonging, encourages community engagement, with an added sprinkle of humour and playfulness. Design elements: logo, sound, slogan; brand look and feel: shape/pattern, colour, naming, typography, space, tone of voice.
- **Benefits:**
  - Functional — an immersive and accessible cinema & entertainment experience.
  - Experiential — the happening place where you can indulge, connect, recharge and leave craving for more.
  - Emotional — the warmth of belonging, the thrill of escapism, and the creation of memorable moments.
  - Cultural — connecting people through the love of big screen storytelling.
  - Societal — educating and supporting the local movie community and championing unheard voices.
- **Brand credentials:** Innovative Momentum (pioneers through committed strategic innovation); Customer Obsessed (fulfilling guests' needs and exceeding expectations); Voice of Entertainment (uniquely positioned to grow the MENA movie industry through exhibiting, distributing, producing and storytelling); Celebrating Moments (bringing people together through cinema — birthdays, occasions, festivals); Differentiating Cinema F&B (best-in-class popcorn and elevated F&B); Largest Cinema Operator in the Region (a wide variety of cinematic experiences in multiple locations).

## Who — brand muse: "The Enthusiasts"
"I am a movie and cinema fanatic! I like to get completely immersed in the cinema experience — from the moment I hear about the latest movie, to seeing my heroes on the big screen. I plan my cinema visit well in advance, specifically choosing the best cinema audiovisual experience, time and date. I take pride in friends/family coming to me for movie recommendations. My leisure time is sacred to me — I want to share it with like-minded people, watching a wide variety of new movies that I love! But I get disappointed when cinema venues fail to pay attention to getting the details right — like allowing others to be disruptive during the movie (too loud/rowdy teens, babies crying), not getting the popcorn right, or screening only mainstream movies — this really spoils my whole cinema experience!"

## How this maps to the VOX Cinemas Virtual Assistant
- Vocabulary already in the agent prompt that matches the house: customers are **guests**; conversational, friendly, bilingual tone; "what's happening" framing suits the recommendation and offers journeys.
- Candidate refinements if the tone is revisited: a light touch of playfulness in greetings/closings; lean on F&B pride (popcorn) in the food journey; position recommendations as *experiences* (MAX, IMAX, GOLD, KIDS) not only titles.
