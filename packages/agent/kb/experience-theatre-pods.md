# THEATRE PODS IN IMAX
## THE THEATRE EXPERIENCE, NOW AVAILABLE IN IMAX
Introducing the world’s most innovative IMAX experience, exclusively at VOX Cinemas Mall of the Emirates!
BOOK TICKETS
Indulge in next-level cinematic luxury. At the new **THEATRE Pods in IMAX**, watch your favourite films in IMAX while enjoying THEATRE’s gourmet menu and unrivalled experience. 
Dine in the exclusive THEATRE Lounge or have your food delivered directly to your seat in the intimate setting of the THEATRE Pods located in the IMAX screen. 
Designed by Ōma Cinema® and developed under the vision of renowned French architect Pierre Chican, the THEATRE Pods combine luxury with cutting-edge cinema technology, offering an unparalleled high-end experience, now available in IMAX at VOX Cinemas Mall of the Emirates. Drawing inspiration from the elegance and atmosphere of traditional theatres, the proprietary Ōma seating layout features a series of tiered balconies, bringing audiences closer to the screen in a vertical arrangement for a more intimate viewing experience. 
Choose from seven THEATRE Pods available, featuring five reclining leather seats, plush blankets and pillows, personalised service, and the fully immersive IMAX experience. 
Book your seat, or for a private experience, book the entire THEATRE Pod in IMAX at VOX Cinemas Mall of the Emirates.

## CONDITIONS OF ENTRY FOR THEATRE Pods in IMAX
- Guests between 5 to 18 years are allowed entry as per the movie restriction but must be accompanied by a guardian or parent. Children under 5 years of age are not permitted inside THEATRE.
- The age rating assigned to the movie should be complied with.
- Food and beverages from any external F&B provider will not be allowed inside the THEATRE lounge and/or cinemas.
