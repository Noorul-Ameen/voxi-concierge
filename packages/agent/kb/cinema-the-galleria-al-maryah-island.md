## Where the cinema is inside the mall
VOX is on the 4th level of The Galleria, above the luxury wing. Take the lifts from the ADGM Square entrance.


## Best parking
Basement parking B2 is closest to the cinema lifts; 3 hours free with validation.

## Accessibility
Fully step-free; accessible seating in IMAX, THEATRE and KIDS.

## About
VOX Cinemas at The Galleria, Al Maryah Island is the go-to cinema in Abu Dhabi. It’s home to the only full laser IMAX in the city along with an upgraded, luxurious THEATRE and a spacious, colourful and exciting KIDS experience. Don’t know what to snack on during your movie? Browse the countless delicious options from Pizza Al Taglio, The Nutella Bar and VOX Cinemas’ homegrown café concept – Kitchen 35. With over 21 screens to choose from, VOX Cinemas at The Galleria, Al Maryah Island can welcome nearly two thousand people at a time. It’s an experience unlike anything you’ve ever seen before.
