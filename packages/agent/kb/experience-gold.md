# GOLD
GOLD is for the film fan who wants to indulge in some extra comfort while enjoying their movie. We’ve created an enticing menu for you to savour while you relax in our luxurious cinema environment.
Our aim is simple: to offer our guests a diverse menu that features a combination of popular, modern flavours coupled with your favourite cinema classics. All our GOLD dishes are presented in our signature VOX Cinemas’ style. We hope you enjoy your food and the movie.

- **Conditions of Entry:**
- Guests between 5 to 18 years are allowed entry as per the movie restriction but must be accompanied by a guardian or parent. Children under 5 years of age are not permitted inside GOLD.
- Please check the classification of the movie. The age rating assigned to the movie should be complied with.
- Food and beverages from any external F&B provider will not be allowed inside the GOLD lounge and/or cinemas.
