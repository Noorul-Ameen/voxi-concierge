## Where the cinema is inside the mall
VOX is on Level 3 (top floor) of Nakheel Mall. Take the panoramic lifts from the Palm Monorail entrance.


## Best parking
Level 3 parking connects directly to the cinema foyer.

## Accessibility
Step-free access; wheelchair seats in every screen.
