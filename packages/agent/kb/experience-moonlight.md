# VOX MOONLIGHT
#### DUBAI'S MOST LOVED OPEN-AIR CINEMA IS NOW OPEN
Dubai’s most loved open-air cinema has been reimagined and is open for the winter season following a fresh makeover.
BOOK TICKETS
Escape into movie magic while enjoying the latest blockbusters under the glittering sky at VOX MOONLIGHT, right on the rooftop of Galleria Mall on Al Wasl Road.
Taking your movie experience to new heights, VOX MOONLIGHT offers a variety of seating options with a prime view of the screen from every seat: bean bags, couches, two person cabanas, or loungers.
Adding to the immersive experience, MOONLIGHT is equipped with the best visual technology and Dolby 7.1 Surround audio for an exhilarating big-screen experience.
To complement your viewing pleasure, order from our wide-ranging F&B menu featuring premium mocktails, sushi, burgers, hotdogs, and all your favourite cinema snacks, right from the comfort of your seat.
VOX MOONLIGHT is open from Monday – Sunday and will screen the biggest blockbusters, so guests can watch Hollywood, Bollywood and Arabic movies like you’ve never seen them before. At the weekend (Friday, Saturday and Sunday), VOX MOONLIGHT will have the first showing of animated and children’s movies and provide a family offering for two adults and two children (one Private Cabana and two Bean Bags) so families can enjoy the magic of movies together.
**Conditions of Entry:** 
- Guests aged between five to 18 years old are allowed entry as per the movie restriction but must be accompanied by a guardian or parent.
- Please check the classification of the movie. The age rating of the movie must be complied with.
- Food and beverage from any external provider will not be allowed inside VOX MOONLIGHT.
