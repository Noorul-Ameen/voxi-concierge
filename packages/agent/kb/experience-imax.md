# IMAX
Clarity, detail and size make IMAX more than just a way to watch movies. Our IMAX cinemas have larger-than-life, curved screens to create a truly immersive experience. 
IMAX's perfectly tuned integrated sound system ensures you can hear a pin drop and be able to tell exactly where it landed. Two projectors run simultaneously to provide life-like clarity to every frame of every IMAX movie.
