## Where the cinema is inside the mall
VOX is on Level 2 above the food court. Take the lifts near Carrefour or the escalators from the central atrium.
Cinema location: Level 2.

## Best parking
Nearest parking: Mall parking (Al Maktoum Rd and Baniyas Rd entrances); Deira City Centre Metro station is a 3-minute walk.

## Accessibility
Lift access from all parking levels; wheelchair-accessible seating; hearing-loop available at GOLD.
