### Returns Policy
VOX Cinemas account holders can request a credit refund on tickets and F&B purchases up to 30 minutes prior to the scheduled start time. The full amount (plus any associated booking fee) will be credited into your VOX wallet and can be used again on any online purchase at VOX Cinemas. 
Cancellations or changes made to bookings less than 30 minutes prior to the scheduled start time are not eligible for refunds. Unfortunately, VOX Cinemas is unable to refund tickets purchased at the kiosk, discounted tickets (bank or telco) or tickets with F&B purchases that have been activated via Prepare Now or QR code. 
If you would like to exchange your online booking for a different session, please request a credit refund and purchase a new ticket using VOX credit. 
### How to request a Refund
To request a refund, go to “My Account”, view “Past Purchases” and click "Manage Booking". If your purchase is eligible for a refund, a "Refund Booking" button will be visible. Please allow up to 30 minutes for the refund amount to be credited to your VOX Wallet.
