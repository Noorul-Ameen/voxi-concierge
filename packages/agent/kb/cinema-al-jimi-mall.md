## Where the cinema is inside the mall
The cinema is located on Level 1. Follow the 'VOX Cinemas' signs from the main atrium or ask at the mall information desk.
Cinema location: Level 1.

## Best parking
Use the mall car park closest to the cinema entrance; parking is free for cinema guests.

## Accessibility
Step-free access via mall lifts; wheelchair-accessible seating available in every screen (please ask staff for assistance); accessible restrooms nearby.
