## Where the cinema is inside the mall
Take the escalators to Level 2 next to Ski Dubai's viewing gallery; VOX is opposite the Kempinski entrance. From the Carrefour side, follow the signs to 'Cinema'.


## Best parking
Nearest parking: Level 2, East.

## Accessibility
Step-free access via lifts by the East car park; wheelchair seats in every screen; accessible restrooms next to the ticket desk.

## About
Head up to level 2 in the exclusive Mall of the Emirates and you’re in retail heaven. Surrounded by boutiques, shops and great eateries, the VOX cinema complex is the perfect addition to a family day out at the Mall. Drop the kids off to watch the latest Disney animations, while you stroll around the shops in peace!

Covering over 100,000 sq. ft, this is believed to be the largest cinema complex in the whole of the Middle East. 24 screens give you endless choices, showing everything from comedies and kid-friendly films through to fright-fests, action-adventure, or historical dramas.

VOX Cinemas are fitted with the very latest immersive technology, including a laser projection setup that gives you sharper, brighter images and an incredible sensation of being right in the middle of the action.
