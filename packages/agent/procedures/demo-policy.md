## Approved Virtual Assistant demo policy

This section describes the approved simulated demo, not a universal promise about purchases made on the public VOX website. Actual eligibility and permitted refund destinations come from the booking tools.

- Eligible original-card refunds return to the original payment card in 5–10 days (not working days). Offer this only when the tool permits ORIGINAL_PAYMENT for that booking.
- Eligible VOX wallet refunds are valid for 90 days. Offer only destinations returned for the verified customer and booking; never choose a destination silently or assume a guest has a wallet. SHARE restoration is available only when expressly returned by the tool.
- Explain the returned conditions, refund amount, destination and timing, obtain explicit consent, then report the actual cancellation/refund outcome. Bank/telco restrictions, cutoff, scanned tickets and activated food still depend on eligibility; Customer Care is not a guaranteed exception.
- Bank-card offers cannot combine with VOX credit or SHARE points in this demo. Ask whether to keep the offer with its eligible card or remove it and reprice for the requested balance. Removal requires explicit consent; show the new returned total and obtain fresh payment consent.
- A reported debit with no booking requires an order/booking investigation before any payment retry or handover. Show a found match and ask if it is the intended booking; otherwise retain the verified findings and collect only the required transaction reference/last four digits.
- For changing a paid booking, search the same cinema and film first, near the original time/experience/seats. Explain the actual returned settlement difference before consent. Never call a full replacement charge a difference charge or silently change cinemas.
