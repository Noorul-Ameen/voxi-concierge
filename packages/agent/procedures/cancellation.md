# Cancel an identified booking

Check the current authentication with [[tool:get_session_context]] when needed. For a signed-in guest, the cancellation request already authorizes reading their existing bookings: use [[tool:list_my_bookings]] immediately unless the exact booking is already verified. Do not ask permission to look, or request a reference, email or phone number when the account lookup is available. Filter the returned cards by the supplied film/date/experience; ask which only if several still match. For a signed-out guest, use [[tool:find_booking]] and complete its required verification before disclosing details or acting. Never guess from a persona name, substitute a mismatched film or read every booking's details aloud.

Call [[tool:check_cancellation_eligibility]] for that verified booking before stating its eligibility or refund options, even when the guest says it used a bank offer. General policy is not evidence that their booking was checked. If ineligible, stop the cancellation flow: do not prepare cancellation or create a complaint to bypass the refusal. Briefly explain the returned reason and offer Customer Care where supported. Wait for an explicit request for a person or acceptance of that transfer before [[tool:transfer_to_agent]]. A cancellation request alone does not accept human transfer, and staff review does not promise cancellation or refund. A bank-offer restriction cannot be bypassed with wallet credit.

Use [[tool:prepare_cancellation]] to obtain the permitted destinations, amount and conditions. Omit refundMethod if the guest has not chosen: the tool supplies permitted choices, never a silent default. Offer only those returned methods. Do not offer SHARE, an unrelated card or a guest wallet unless eligibility explicitly permits it. If the guest already chose an allowed destination, do not ask again.

A mixed VOX-credit/card purchase does not imply its full value can return to one card. Respect the returned supported refund destinations; if original-payment refund is unavailable, offer only the permitted wallet route or assistance, without promising a card timeline. Policy refusal and handover are not cancellation success: do not mark the cancellation journey completed unless [[tool:cancel_booking]] actually succeeded.

For this approved demo, say an eligible original-card refund takes **5–10 days** / **من 5 إلى 10 أيام**, never working or business days / أيام عمل. VOX wallet credit is valid for 90 days; state that validity when offering it. These are demo rules, not a claim that every real VOX purchase supports a card refund. State the actual method/amount/timeline returned by preparation. If the result contradicts this reference, stop and explain the uncertainty instead of promising the more generous policy.

Present one concise confirmation with the identified booking, refund amount, chosen destination and relevant timing/conditions. Wait for explicit cancellation consent. Only then call [[tool:cancel_booking]] with the current confirmationId and confirmed true. A request to see eligibility, a method preference, or “what happens to my money?” is not cancellation consent.

For queued/running results, use [[tool:get_action_result]] with the returned actionId until the terminal outcome. Do not claim cancellation/refund success on preparation, queuing or an error. On success, acknowledge once and point to the visible result. Do not promise an email or a completed bank credit unless the result confirms it.
